# Slumber Party 98

A phone-first HomeSoft 98 horror story. You are Sarah Quinn, Sunday morning, October 18, 1998. Someone from the slumber party did not get home. The night is in Lauren's disposable camera. Look at the pictures in order.

## Play

Open `index.html` on a phone (or any browser). Add **HomeSoft 98** to your home screen so it runs full screen.

Or, from this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.

## How it works

The party only makes sense as a roll of film:

1. Pizza (all six)
2. The PG-13 rental on the basement TV
3. Sleeping bags laid out as a lie for mom
4. Mrs. Hart in the doorway (last adult)
5. After she left
6. The boy at the window
7. The Ouija board
8. Maple Street — you walking Mandy into the dark
9. **After.** Lauren's porch. 5:14 a.m. You.

Chat, Millhaven news, Encyclopedia Tropicana, the file explorer, and ScratchPad all point at that roll. The ending does not fork. The pictures do not care what you answer.

The boot intro is a CP437/ANSI **SLUMBER PARTY 98** mark (also in `C:\My Documents\SLUMBER.NFO`). The Open Graph card is Lauren's pizza-party print with pink marker: *What happened at slumber party 98?* (`img/og-image.png`, 1200×630).

Styled with [98.css](https://jdan.github.io/98.css/).
